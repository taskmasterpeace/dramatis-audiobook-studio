THE PRODUCTION LINE

Every beat has
a department.

Your story travels through a real production pipeline. Every stage is inspectable, auditionable, and yours to direct.

  1. 01
    C

    Compile

    Structure the manuscript into scenes, speakers, and cues while preserving every source citation.

  2. 02
    C

    Cast

    Audition voices against age, register, accent, and the character you actually wrote.

  3. 03
    P

    Perform

    Route each line to the right performer—from fast local narration to directed hero moments.

  4. 04
    S

    Sound Design

    Place approved effects on the word that triggers them and hold ambience under the scene.

  5. 05
    M

    Mix

    Balance four independent stems with dialogue-safe ducking and editable scene control.

  6. 06
    M

    Master

    Deliver immersive and clean masters, bound into a chaptered production ready to publish.
