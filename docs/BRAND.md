# Audio Movie Studio Brand Identity

## Brand

- **Name:** Audio Movie Studio
- **Compact identifier:** `audiomoviestudio`
- **Status:** Closed beta
- **Promise:** Turn a manuscript into a movie for your ears.
- **Supporting line:** Cast every character. Direct every line. Score every scene.
- **Positioning:** A local-first production studio for full-cast cinematic audio—not a generic text-to-speech tool.

Use the full name on first mention. `Audio Movie Studio` is always three words and always title case in prose. The compact identifier is reserved for commands, package names, and machine-readable contexts.

## Sonic Frame mark

The identity is built around the **Sonic Frame**: a cut-corner cinematic frame containing a five-bar waveform. The frame represents direction and composition; the waveform represents voice, sound design, and score. Cyan corner cuts imply a production timeline moving through the frame. The amber dot is the single closed-beta/live-recording signal.

Assets:

- `studio/shared/logo-mark.svg` — icon, favicon, and compact UI use
- `studio/shared/logo-horizontal.svg` — wide brand lockup
- `studio/shared/logo-stacked.svg` — square and sidebar lockup
- `installer/audio-movie-studio.ico` — Windows application icon

Keep clear space around the mark equal to one waveform-bar width. Do not place the mark on cyan, stretch it, recolor it purple, add a play triangle, or separate the amber signal dot from the frame.

## Color

| Role | Value | Use |
|---|---:|---|
| Deep stage | `#080C10` | Primary background |
| Carbon | `#0D1116` | Mark and dark surfaces |
| Panel graphite | `#121A20` | Raised production surfaces |
| Warm silver | `#E7ECEC` | Primary type and frame highlights |
| Muted steel | `#8B9BA3` | Supporting copy |
| Signal cyan | `#3EC5CF` | Action, waveform, live production state |
| Bright cyan | `#7AF1ED` | Controlled highlights only |
| Beta amber | `#D99A3D` | Closed-beta and recording signals only |

Purple is never part of the Audio Movie Studio identity.

## Typography and voice

League Gothic is the cinematic display face. Manrope is the readable interface and body face. Display copy is short, declarative, and set like a film title card. Body copy is precise and production-minded.

Say **cast**, **direct**, **scene**, **performance**, **soundstage**, and **master**. Avoid breathless AI language, magic-button claims, fake metrics, and wording that reduces the product to narration generation.
